Eataly

Omnichannel Innovation & AI Navigation

01 / The Vision

Reconnecting with Meaning

Modern consumers demand transparency that transforms ingredients from commodities into living narratives of heritage. We chose Eataly to bridge the gap between strong culinary tradition and the need for digital seamlessness.

MARIO AI

02 / The Challenge

The Engagement Gap

Gen-Z recognizes Eataly’s cultural relevance but suffers from low purchase frequency and decision fatigue when navigating the complex physical store.

  • Consumer Mantra: "Quality I can trace, technology I can trust".
  • Barriers: Feeling overwhelmed by choices and a limited in-store experience.
03 / The New Solution

AI Totems & Human Connection

Our solution features AI totems that act as personal navigators. Technology acts as a silent assistant, personalizing the journey without becoming an intrusive barrier.

  • Transparency: Users scan products to see the entire supply chain, from origin to store.
  • Navigation: Totems provide real-time paths to items and seasonal recipe suggestions.
  • Human Touch: Digital guidance leads shoppers to specialized employees, empowering staff as cultural stewards.
04 / Infinite Journey

Seamless Integration

The experience begins pre-store with web browsing and pre-orders, moving into an exploratory in-store phase guided by AI and human interaction.

The Customer Loop

Browsing → Exploring → Interaction → Human Connection → Post-Purchase Learning

05 / Expected Impact

Redefining the Product

Retail transcends price wars to compete on trust[cite: 98]. Products are no longer just goods, but carriers of culture.

  • For the Customer: A sense of clarity, personalization, and confidence in their moral and ethical choices.
  • For the Employee: Empowerment as an "Expert" with purpose, providing the cultural depth that completes the digital journey.
