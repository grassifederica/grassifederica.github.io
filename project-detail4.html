01 / THE BRIEF

Stealth Storytelling

As part of the Italian Way 2026 challenge at POLIMI GSoM, we created a social media channel to document university masterclasses[cite: 1, 2]. Under strict non-disclosure rules, we were barred from using any official brand names, logos, corporate colors, or copyrighted images[cite: 1, 2].

The Subversion
We took "Pizza Pasta Mandolino"—the ultimate Italian stereotype—and wrapped it in a sleek, luxury-coded vector identity[cite: 1].
Gamification
Instead of giving answers, we posted questions[cite: 1]. Every carousel invited followers to "Guess the Brand" based on its DNA[cite: 1, 2].
Active Reach
By making posts enigmatic and interactive, followers engaged, commented, and shared to check if their guess was right[cite: 2].
02 / VISUAL ARCHITECTURE

The Flag Grid Concept

Every carousel was assigned one color of the Italian flag[cite: 1, 2]. When viewed as a 3-column Instagram feed, the entire profile slowly assembled into a unified national flag[cite: 1, 2].
