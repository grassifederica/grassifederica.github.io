01 / THE BRIEF

Stealth Storytelling

As part of the Italian Way 2026 challenge at POLIMI GSoM, we created a social media channel to document university masterclasses . Under strict non-disclosure rules, we were barred from using any official brand names, logos, corporate colors, or copyrighted images .

The Subversion
We took "Pizza Pasta Mandolino"—the ultimate Italian stereotype—and wrapped it in a sleek, luxury-coded vector identity .
Gamification
Instead of giving answers, we posted questions . Every carousel invited followers to "Guess the Brand" based on its DNA .
Active Reach
By making posts enigmatic and interactive, followers engaged, commented, and shared to check if their guess was right .
02 / VISUAL ARCHITECTURE

The Flag Grid Concept

Every carousel was assigned one color of the Italian flag . When viewed as a 3-column Instagram feed, the entire profile slowly assembled into a unified national flag .

Strategic Key Learnings
• Carousels Reward Depth: Story-led reveals held attention far better than single images .
• Grid Continuity Builds Trust: The flag palette made the feed instantly recognizable .
• English Language Impact: Expanded reach to design-conscious expats globally .
• Gamification Drive: Question hooks turned passive scrolling into active participation .
