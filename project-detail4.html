01 / The Concept

Pizza Pasta Mandolino

Created from scratch for the Italian Way 2026 project, @ppm.culture playfully subverts the classic Italian tropes listed in our bio: Pizza, Pasta, Mandolino[cite: 1, 2]. By wrapping a deeply traditional concept in an ultra-sleek, minimalist vector aesthetic, we engaged Gen-Z and Millennial audiences with high-end luxury storytelling[cite: 1].

  • Brief: Build an original Instagram profile from zero to document university guest lectures[cite: 1, 2].
  • Rule: Strict prohibition against using real brand names, official logos, brand colors, or direct assets[cite: 1, 2].
  • Mechanic: Gamified carousels inviting followers to "Guess the Brand" based on key DNA triggers[cite: 1, 2].
02 / The Visual Architecture

The Italian Flag Grid

We engineered a strategic posting architecture where every carousel cover was assigned a specific color[cite: 1, 2]. viewed as a whole, the Instagram grid gradually revealed the Italian flag pattern to reward curious profile visitors[cite: 1, 2].

03 / Target Audience[cite: 1]

Catering to Design Connoisseurs

Primary focus on Gen Z and young Millennials (Ages 16–28) located in Italian creative hubs like Milan, expanding globally to design-conscious expats[cite: 1].

  • Demographics: Creative directors, architects, fashion students, and digital creators[cite: 1].
  • Audience Traits: They follow accounts for aesthetic curation and appreciate editorial design and typography[cite: 1].
  • Tone of Voice: Bold, minimal, and provocative—where every word earns its place[cite: 2].
