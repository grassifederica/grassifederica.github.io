The Green Thread

A celebration of
Bottega Green —
seen not as a colour,
but as a blueprint.

Born in 1966 in Vicenza, Bottega Veneta built its identity on one principle: the human hand as the highest tool. The Green Thread is a global odyssey connecting the ancient art of Intrecciato with modern sustainability — a promise to the earth told by the hands of those who shape it.

The Green Thread · Concept Statement

We don't just wear the future.
We weave it.

CRAFT
Craft in Motion
Impactful Event Concept

The ancient art
of Intrecciato
performed live.

In each boutique, master artisans perform live weaving using upcycled leather — their hands becoming a performance illustrating the "Craft in Motion" philosophy. Guests participate in tactile experiments, learning the history of the weave that defines the house.

Invitation Design

Recycled paper.
Leather scent.
Heritage in hand.

A6 sheets of recycled paper with leather texture on one side. A leather-like frame with stitching on the other — the heavy stitch symbolising craftsmanship, the scented paper connecting modernity with heritage. A strip of leather scrap from past collections threaded through a punched hole creates an accessory guests keep.

Federica Grassi · Polimi GSOM 2025
Project Author

By merging heritage
with strategy, we craft
something timeless.

This project was developed as part of the Event Lab Business Game at Polimi GSOM, under the direction of Prof. Manuela Balli. The brief: design a global event plan for the launch of Bottega Veneta's SS collection. The result: a concept rooted in the brand's deepest values — craft, sustainability, and the silent luxury of things made to last.
